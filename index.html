PRIVACY & RESEARCH

Your answers, explained.

We collect the eight answers you submit to evaluate a stroller concept. Providing an email is optional. If you provide one, you must opt in to concept updates and research invitations.

Responses are stored in Supabase and are accessible to the research team, not other visitors. We do not ask for children’s names or dates of birth. We do not sell your email or use advertising trackers.

You can leave before submitting; your answers stay only in this page’s memory and are discarded when you reload or close it. After submission, responses are retained for up to 12 months. You can request deletion or unsubscribe by replying to a research email.

This is early concept research, not an offer for sale. In preview mode, nothing is sent or saved.
